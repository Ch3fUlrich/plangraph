V2 — check_rev completeness / remaining argument injection.

`check_rev` (src/git.rs:20) rejects empty, leading `-`, whitespace, and control chars — closing the option-like and multi-token rev classes. Every rev-bearing call is now guarded: `confine::changed_files` and `confine::diff_hash` (src/confine.rs:80,121), `janitor::commits_beyond_branch` (src/janitor.rs:104), `probe::commits_beyond` (src/probe.rs:29), and `worktree::create` (src/worktree.rs:59) additionally places dest+base after `--`.

Card-derived branch names are not an injection vector: in `worktree::create` the branch is the value of `-b` (consumed as its operand, never re-parsed as a flag), and in `janitor::commits_beyond_branch` the rev is the composite `{base}..{branch}` whose leading token is the guarded `base`, so even a hostile `branch` suffix cannot produce an argv starting with `-`. No fetch/push refspec call sites take user revs. The `--all`/`--output=` cases are caught. Residual nit (non-blocking): `check_rev` permits `+` and `@{...}` forms, but these are valid rev syntax, not option injection. No remaining injection path found.

V4 — Deferred symlink and the two noted gaps.

Symlink escape: correctly out of this crate. Git-diff confinement cannot observe writes through a committed symlink to outside the repo; that requires an OS/filesystem sandbox in the execution harness (glue lane). Deferral to RT-P1-WORKER/harness is the right place, not a core-crate bug.

24h wait cap never parks + unbounded probe_fail backoff: acceptable to defer the *side effects* (park + alarm are Temporal glue), and the writer explicitly logged both as glue TODOs in REVIEW-R2.md. One caveat worth flagging for the next lane: `budget::next_action` is pure and stateless per call, so it cannot itself count repeated waits; the glue must own the wait/backoff attempt counter, and it must not silently reinvent budget arithmetic. Recommend the glue add (or the core later expose) a bounded `Park` decision so the "capped at 24h then park" clause has a single source of truth. Not a fix-in-this-crate blocker.
