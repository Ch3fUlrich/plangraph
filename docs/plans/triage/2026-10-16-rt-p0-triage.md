## Method

- Input: every open item of the three projects in the keeper snapshot of 2026-10-16 20:34Z (448 items).
- Basis: `docs/plans/2026-10-15-rethink-setup-design.md` (D-1285) and the orchestration-setup design (S1-S23).
- Four read-only classification passes (one per chunk), then a merge and a review by this L1. Verdicts:
  - CANCEL: superseded or duplicated; the reason names what supersedes it.
  - REPARENT: real work that a rethink phase needs; joins that RT gate.
  - INDEPENDENT: unrelated to the rethink, still valid, or in progress (claimed/in_review never cancelled).
  - COVERED: already handled by the orch-setup batch or already a dep of an RT gate.
- Rule: a wrong cancel is worse than a missed one. Unsure items stay INDEPENDENT and are listed under Doubts.
- The batch is built on the graph as it will be after the orch-setup batch. Every live edge to a cancelled item is dropped,
  so nothing dangles. Checks: no missing deps, no dangling deps, no cycles.
