`plangraph landing report OWNER/REPO --last N` reads the stage timeline of the newest landed trains from the keeper database (new `land` action `recent`, ordered by merge time) and names the critical path, the bottleneck stage per train and the slowest stage over the last N trains; a train without a required stage or without telemetry is marked incomplete, and the time to merge ends at the merge stage. Stage rows carry host, result and the push-gate family (new `landing_stages` columns, added to existing databases on open), written by the pre-push hook, preflight, the lander and `plangraph review`. It replaces the local `land-timeline` ledger.
