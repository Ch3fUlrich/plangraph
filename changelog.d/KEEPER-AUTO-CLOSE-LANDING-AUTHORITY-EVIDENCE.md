The automatic merge close now records a `landing` object in its commit evidence: the standing operator decision (config `merge_close_decision`), the pre-merge required-check verdict of each landed PR head read from the forge once per landing, and the GO rows that bind a landed PR. A squash merge closes with the PR head the forge reports. A missing standing decision, unverified checks or a missing GO row are flagged (`landing_flags`) and never refuse a proved landing.
