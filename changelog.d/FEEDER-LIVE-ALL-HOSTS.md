The live writer feeder admits by free RAM: a host's writer slots are (free RAM - floor) / RAM per writer, load only paces a saturated host to one new writer per tick, and the capacity line of every decision shows the numbers.
The shipped feeder policy lists the workstation beside coding and dell, and a host the policy does not list refuses as unknown_host:<name>, also in live mode.
A feeder host without its own quota reader reads the center's quota snapshot file (written by `plangraph quota --export`); a missing or older-than-15-minutes snapshot refuses as quota_stale and names its age.
Live fleet-launch writer sessions (hand lanes) count as running writers on their host and hold the plan items their card names, so hand lanes and feeder runs never overlap.
