The scheduled review probe now reads an OpenCode leg's usage-limit reply (HTTP 429/402 error event) the way a real run does, books the leg limited with its reset time and skips it on the next run, so a limited OpenCode leg no longer makes `plangraph-review-probe.service` exit 1 every 15 minutes. Any skipped row stays out of the sweep verdict; a failed leg still exits 1 (plangraph:LE-REVIEW-PROBE-UNIT-FAILS-ON-LIMITED-LEGS).
