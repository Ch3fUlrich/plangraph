- The fleet launcher keeps a state-shadow role attached to its live session: a role with a collector (`state-shadow/ROLE.env`) gets its own settings file `launch-settings/role-ROLE.json` (lean profile, caps and the measure-only hook, rewritten at every launch, never shared with a role of equal caps), and every launch rewrites `ROLE.json` with the new session id and its transcript under any Claude project directory. The collector re-reads session and transcript each scan, refreshes its quota file from the live quota snapshots each scan and fails the quota source when that file is older than ten minutes. `host-sync --install-env state-shadow` generates `ROLE.json` from `ROLE.seed.json` and the role's launch record.
