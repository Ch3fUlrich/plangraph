`merge_batch.py go-req` runs `land-train deploy-plan` over the batch parts with the hosts registry and the host install dry run, and puts the `deploy:` line and one `READY` or `NOT READY ... blocker KEY (MISSING ...)` block per family and host into the GO-REQ; the blocker items are filed with `pg define --file`. `deploy-plan` takes `--spans` and `--registry`, prints those blocks, and a family shared by two parts has one blocker with its reasons listed once.
