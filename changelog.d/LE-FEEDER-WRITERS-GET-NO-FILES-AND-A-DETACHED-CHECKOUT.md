- **LE-FEEDER-WRITERS-GET-NO-FILES-AND-A-DETACHED-CHECKOUT:** The feeder takes only items with a spec card (`docs/specs/<ID>.card.yaml`) and gives the writer that card's write set; items without one are counted as `needs_spec`. A host starts its next writer only when its running feeder writers have committed (`pace_by_commit`), and stops while three of its last five ended feeder runs added no commit (`no_commit_stop`, `no_commit_window`).
