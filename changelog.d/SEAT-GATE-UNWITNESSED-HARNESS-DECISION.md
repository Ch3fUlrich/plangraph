A merged close counts a seat whose served model is `unwitnessed` (agy Hydrogen, rule 3.26) when the run record names its harness and the chosen leg's model and family, the verdict names the exact head and a file read, and an opencode run reports tests that ran. Lanes with an `auth` or `secrets` risk tag still refuse it.
A train landing (`require_train_review`) also counts a completed seat whose `override_reason` or pin names only decisions of one allowlist (D-4487, D-5903, D-6804), with a recorded harness, chosen-leg family and served model, an explicit ACCEPT and a verdict on the exact part head from the completion; only for a part outside authentication, secrets and gate files (the keeper diffs the part against the train base), any doubt keeps the refusal.
An override seat of a train landing is also refused when any item of the part is tagged authentication or secrets, and a train prepared again on another base checks its override seats again.
