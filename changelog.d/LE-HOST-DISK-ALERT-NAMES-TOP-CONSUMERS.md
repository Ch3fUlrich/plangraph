Host disk alerts are keyed by filesystem: each crossing above 85% used queues one measured alert naming the five largest directories of that filesystem, optionally after a bare one, and recovers only on a sample of the same filesystem at or below 85%. Collectors may supply keyed `disks` samples with consumers; the local host reads `disk-watermarks.json` rows by device, and only rows measured after the last observed recovery. Deploy: no deploy needed beyond the next watchdog and `pg-disk-hygiene` timer pass.
