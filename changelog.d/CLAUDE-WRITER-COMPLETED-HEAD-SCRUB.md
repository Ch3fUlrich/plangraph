A completed Claude batch session is reported only after its head passes the shared head gate on the admitted base: the lifecycle recovery that turns the roster's finished session into a run outcome runs `head_gate::run` in the session's working directory, turns a rejected head into a failed run (`reason: head_gate_rejected`) and keeps the gate diagnostic as `head_gate` on the record and in the outcome witness. The admitted base is the typed input's `run.base` pinned to a commit at launch (a base that names no commit refuses the launch), never text scanned from a brief. A respawn carries `item_keys`, `lane_override` and `admitted_base` of the launch it continues. The fleet session record keeps them and `cwd`.
