A completed Claude batch session is reported only after its head passes the shared head gate on the admitted base: the lifecycle recovery that turns the roster's finished session into a run outcome runs `head_gate::run` in the session's working directory, turns a rejected head into a failed run (`reason: head_gate_rejected`) and keeps the gate diagnostic as `head_gate` on the record (and in the outcome witness). The fleet session record keeps `admitted_base` and `cwd` next to `item_keys`.
