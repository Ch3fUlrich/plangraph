Per-host leg truth: `host_served_legs` turns a host's own model listing and tiny-call failures into `served` or `not served` with a reason per leg (`not_listed`, `harness_missing`, `managed_service_port_conflict`, the probe's reason), `served_digest` prints one `legs served per host` line, and `overflow.decide(leg=...)` never places a leg on a host whose record says it is not served. A managed-service port conflict (`opencode serve --service` holding the port) is its own reason in discovery and in the leg probe, no longer `exit 1`.
