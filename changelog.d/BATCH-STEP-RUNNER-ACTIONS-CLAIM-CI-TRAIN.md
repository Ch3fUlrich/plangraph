The batch step runner has real claim, ci_class and train_join actions (`step_services`): claim runs the keeper `claim-batch` for every item and fails with every key released when one is refused, ci_class reads the workflow runs of the pushed head and classifies them green or red (waiting while a run is pending), and train_join passes the consumed CI run to the site's train desk command. The service calls are traits with command-backed implementations, so a fake keeper and CI run a batch to train join in tests.
