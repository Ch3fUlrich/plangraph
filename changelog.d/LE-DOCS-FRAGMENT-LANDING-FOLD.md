`plangraph land-train fold-docs` folds the per-item documentation fragments (`docs/contributing|keeper|spawner/<ITEM>.md`) into their shared reference once per train, in name order, without rewriting existing text or passing the reference's line cap, and `--measure` counts direct edits of the shared references in a range. The post-merge settlement runs the fold as its last step on the landed merge commit and commits it on the branch `land/docs-fold-<train>` without touching the checkout; CONTRIBUTING.md and docs/SPAWNER.md were reflowed to get room under their unchanged caps.
