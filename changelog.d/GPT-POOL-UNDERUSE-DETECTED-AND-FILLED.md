- **GPT-POOL-UNDERUSE-DETECTED-AND-FILLED (readout):** `plangraph quota pool [--db PATH]` prints the ChatGPT pool per window as JSON: for each window the used percent, observation time, reset and coverage of the accounts whose newest snapshot is fresh and still inside its window. The newest observation of each provider, account and window wins before any freshness or validity check, so an invalid newest row never lets an older one stand in. The snapshot source has no account roster, so the averages describe only the observed usable accounts and `coverage.full_pool` stays "unknown"; rows without an account identity are counted apart and never averaged. An account counts only when its newest observation lies inside its window and after the window began (the reset minus the window length); observations, resets and the clock are compared to the nanosecond, and a zone-less ISO text with a `T` or a space is read as UTC. A malformed stored value (a BLOB, an infinite number) leaves only its own cell unknown. Pace is the mean of each account's own elapsed fraction at its own observation time, over the same accounts as the usage average. A source with no identifiable windows reports `status: unknown`. `plangraph quota` itself is unchanged. No deploy needed: a CLI readout, nothing is scheduled.
