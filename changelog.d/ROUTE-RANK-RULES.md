The route applies utility eligibility and score rules: a leg lacking a card need (`needs`: tools, shell, long_context, vision; `tool_mode`) is skipped as `needs_<need>` (fallback legs pass the same filter); an effort the row's `effort_channel` cannot pass is skipped as `effort_channel`, never lowered; an imputed row ranks at mean minus 1 sd and never above a measured row of equal mean; quality is the own p_hat at n >= 20, else the coding index, else the AA index; a row older than 45 days or after an index_version, price or served-model change is skipped as `stale_row` for high and critical cards, and a new own row needs a 5-card canary (`canary_pending`) (plangraph:ROUTE-RANK-RULES).
