A push of a `refs/heads/lane/*` ref runs the public scrub, the commit gate (with its single open-PR query) and the stamp gate only: no worktree is created and no preflight or test runs, so two lane pushes from two worktrees of one clone cannot touch each other's worktrees. The batch-writer skill tells a writer with a `PUSH:` line to push `lane/<NAME>` after every item commit with its receipt in one atomic push (`git push --atomic origin HEAD:refs/heads/lane/<NAME> +refs/notes/plangraph-fast-checks:refs/notes/plangraph-fast-checks-lane-<NAME>`); the hook validates and scrubs the per-lane receipt ref and refuses a lane head whose green receipt is not in the same push, there is no bypass flag. The native writer and the step runner produce and push the same receipt. `plangraph wait lane-alert --status FILE` raises `ALERT lane-head-not-on-remote` for a `<NAME> DONE` status whose head is not on the remote after 10 minutes.
