A push of a `refs/heads/lane/*` ref runs the public scrub, the commit gate (with its single open-PR query) and the stamp gate only: no worktree is created and no preflight or test runs, so two lane pushes from two worktrees of one clone cannot touch each other's worktrees. The batch-writer skill tells a writer with a `PUSH:` line to push `lane/<NAME>` after every item commit with its receipt in one atomic push (the brief's `PUSH:` line: the head and `+refs/plangraph/receipts/<head>` as the lane's designated `refs/notes/plangraph-fast-checks-<lane>`, a name that is injective in the lane). A receipt is one parentless commit holding only that receipt, so nothing else is published with it; the hook validates and scrubs it, refuses a receipt ref that is not the designated one of a lane head in the same push, and refuses a lane head without its green receipt; there is no bypass flag. The native writer runs the shared `plangraph fast-checks --owned-worktree` (no receipt of its own), and it and the step runner push the same receipt; the feeder card names the lane the host pushes to. `plangraph wait lane-alert --status FILE` raises `ALERT lane-head-not-on-remote` for a `<NAME> DONE` status whose head is not on the remote after 10 minutes. A receipt commit must be canonical (one fixed author, committer and message, flat `<head>` path), so no text rides in its metadata; only lane pushes carry a receipt ref (phase and other branches are pushed alone).
