A push of a `refs/heads/lane/*` ref runs the public scrub, the commit gate and the stamp gate only: no worktree is created, no preflight and no network call runs, so two lane pushes from two worktrees of one clone cannot touch each other's worktrees. The batch-writer skill tells a writer with a `PUSH:` line to push `lane/<NAME>` after every item commit (`PG_LANE_PUSH_UNSTAMPED=1` for an intermediate commit; the final head needs a green stamp). `plangraph wait lane-alert --status FILE` raises `ALERT lane-head-not-on-remote` for a `<NAME> DONE` status whose head is not on the remote after 10 minutes.
